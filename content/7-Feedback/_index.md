## A Few Thoughts After the Internship

### Overall experience
Before joining FCJ Cloud Intern, I only knew AWS from classroom theory. The past 12 weeks were the first time I actually got my hands dirty building and running a system on the cloud end-to-end — from designing the database, writing the API, to finally seeing my SmartDorm project running live on Lambda instead of just sitting on my own laptop.

### Satisfaction level
* **Personal rating:** 5/5
* What I liked most was getting to decide the technical architecture for my own project instead of just following a set path, even though it was a bit intimidating at first having to choose between options on my own (e.g. whether to use EC2 or Lambda).

### Things I think could be improved
* In the early phase, I spent a fair amount of time figuring out IAM policies and permissions for Lambda on my own — a dedicated workshop on "permissions for serverless apps" would probably save future interns some of that struggle.
* Since I focused almost entirely on the Backend, my exposure to the frontend side was fairly limited — a short reference guide for backend-leaning interns who want to get up to speed on frontend quickly would be helpful.

### Would I recommend this program
Absolutely. It's a rare opportunity for a student to work with real cloud infrastructure, with mentors available when needed but still enough room to make your own technical decisions — something that's hard to get from classroom learning alone.
